Coldzera

Coldzera is a Brazilian CS:GO player known for his mechanical skill and clutch plays. He became especially famous for his legendary jumping AWP play on Mirage. His aggressive style and confidence made him one of the most recognizable players in CS:GO. He is remembered for several iconic tournament moments.

AWP

The AWP is a powerful sniper rifle in CS:GO. It deals very high damage and is mainly used for long-range fights. It is expensive, so losing it can be costly for the team. Skilled players can use the AWP to control important areas of the map and get quick kills.

Info

One of Coldzera’s most famous plays happened on Mirage near the car position. He jumped with an AWP and hit an incredible no-scope shot. He continued the play and finished with four kills. The moment became one of the most iconic highlights in CS:GO history.
